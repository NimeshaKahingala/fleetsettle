# QA findings — 23 August 2026

Requested directly: verify four basic business scenarios work end to end, live, before a larger test push. Not a catalogue sweep — a targeted walkthrough of one realistic business story, grounded in the spec first, then run against `qa.fleetsettle.com` with a fresh vehicle built for this test alone.

## The four scenarios

1. Register a car and/or bus — the bus is on a daily lease by default.
2. The same bus occasionally goes on multi-day trips (2–25 days), each with an agreed amount, and a driver fee is paid for the trip.
3. An incident or breakdown during a trip is recorded and its cost borne by the business, not the customer.
4. A manager who forgot to confirm the daily amount for a few days can confirm or act on each day separately.

## Test vehicle

`QA-SCEN-BUS1` (Bus, daily lease, driver `QA Driver 0808052656` at Rs 3,500/day, effective 23 Aug 2026 ongoing) — created for this session, left in place afterward the same way other `QA-*` fixtures already live in this business.

## 1. Register a car/bus, default to daily lease — confirmed clean

`Add a vehicle`'s form pre-selects Bus + Daily lease with no other input, and saves on Registration + Type + Arrangement alone (U-2 held). Checked whether the arrangement default is vehicle-type-aware by switching to Car mid-form: the arrangement selection did not change, confirming the two fields are genuinely independent, matching CLAUDE.md's own framing ("the dividing line is not car vs bus"). Switched back to Bus, saved cleanly, no console errors. Starting the actual daily lease (driver, pattern, amount, effective date) is a separate action (`Start a daily lease`) — also level-1-only, also clean.

## 2. Multi-day charter on an already-leased bus — confirmed correct, after GAP-171

Booked a real 5-day trip (26–30 Aug) on `QA-SCEN-BUS1` while its daily lease was active. The confirmation step showed exactly what F-5.1 promises: *"This pauses 5 days of the daily lease — its expected income (Rs 17,500) disappears"* (5 × Rs 3,500, exact). Driver defaulted to a picker (not literally pre-filled, but the lease driver was one tap away and selected); driver trip fee (Rs 15,000) and agreed amount (Rs 45,000) both landed on the booked trip correctly. Cross-checked in the database: the trip row carries both figures exactly, `day_record`s for 26–30 Aug never got created in the first place (the daily lease's own materialisation is what would have written them, and the trip's booking logic correctly avoided doing so for dates still inside this session's own closed-period gap — see the note at the end of this document).

**GAP-171, found getting the agreed amount in.** Three separate attempts to open the "Agreed amount" field on Step 1 — the wizard's own last field — silently advanced to Step 2 instead of opening the amount sheet, discarding whatever the manager intended to enter. Traced to a genuine layout defect: `Screen`'s sticky bottom CTA overlaps the tail of a form whose content overflows the viewport, and a plain tap in the overlapping band hits the CTA, not the field, because `scroll-padding-bottom` only affects scroll-into-view targeting for an already-focused element, not static hit-testing before any focus event. Proven with `document.elementFromPoint` at the field's own centre resolving to the "Next" button, and with matching bounding-rect overlap (23px). Full account and exact numbers in `TRACKER.md`'s own GAP-171 row. Worked around by scrolling the container to its end before tapping, which let the amount sheet open normally and confirmed the diagnosis. **Filed, not fixed.**

## 3. Incident/breakdown during the trip, borne by us — borne-by correct, GAP-172 found

Reported a real incident on `QA-SCEN-BUS1` ("Engine breakdown en route to Kandy, roadside repair needed," dated inside the trip). Recorded a real Rs 8,000 "Repairs" expense on the same vehicle, dated inside the trip window. **The borne-by half of this scenario is exactly right**: with no manual override, the expense's `borne_by` column resolved to `us` — matching UC §6.7's matrix, where Repairs is "Us"/"Ours" across all three arrangements, so a breakdown during a charter never lands on the customer by default.

**GAP-172, found looking for where the cost attaches to the incident.** There is no such place. The incident's own "Incident actions" menu offers only "Record off-road days," "Customer contribution" and "Submit insurance claim" — no cost-recording action at all. The trip's own detail screen offers only "Record advance" and "Close/Cancel trip" — also nothing. The generic "Record expense" form (the only place a cost can actually be entered) has no incident or trip picker anywhere, including under "More." Checked the schema and the API to see whether this was a display-only gap: `expense.trip_id` and `expense.incident_id` both exist and are fully handled by `api/src/handlers/expense.ts` (validated, stored, returned, filterable) — the backend is completely ready, nothing in the client ever calls it. Confirmed live rather than only by source: the Rs 8,000 repair sits on the vehicle's own cost list correctly, but the trip's "Costs so far" stayed **Rs 0** and the incident's "Total repairs" stayed **Rs 0**, both permanently, since nothing ever set either id and there is no way to go back and set it after the fact either. **Filed, not fixed** — this is a bigger gap than the one scenario it was found through: every trip's own P&L (F-5.4) and every incident's own bottom line (UC-12) are structurally stuck at zero cost, for any business using this build today.

## 4. Catching up on forgotten days — confirmed clean, including a near-miss

Confirmed three separate pending days individually, each with its own tap, none touching the others: NC-1234's Sat 22 Aug (a real day left over from an earlier session), NC-1234's Sun 23 Aug, and `QA-SCEN-BUS1`'s own first day (Sun 23 Aug, created on demand — see below). All three now read "Confirmed" independently on Home, matching F-4.2's per-day model directly (this also revisits and reconfirms the same write-atomicity property this session's Part 2 pass already established, at 100% consistency across a third instance).

**One case looked like a bug, was half right on the first pass, and turned out to be GAP-173 once the owner pushed back on it directly.** `QA-SCEN-BUS1`'s own daily-lease `day_record`s were never materialised for 23–31 August at all — only for 1–30 September — because this QA business's currently open accounting period is September while "today" is still 23 August (see the note below). Confirming the vehicle's own "today" card anyway (Home shows a derivable card even with no persisted row, per F-4.2's own "confirming a day with no card generates it first") succeeded, and the resulting `day_record`'s `posted_period_id` pointed at **September** — which looked, at first glance, like money landing in the wrong month. Checked `resolvePeriodLinkage` (`api/src/queries/accounting-period.ts`) directly: `posted_period_id` is deliberately the *currently open* period, since a closed one can no longer accept writes, while a separate `belongs_to_period_id` is set whenever the two differ — and on this row, `belongs_to_period_id` correctly pointed at **August**. This is the write-side half of the "facts arrive late" (W-35) mechanism working exactly as designed, and the first pass at this document called that the whole story: "working exactly as designed, not a bug."

**It wasn't the whole story.** Asked directly why a confirmed day shouldn't just belong to the day it's for, re-reading `user-flows.md` F-8.1's own Accept clause answered it: *"reports for the closed month are unchanged; reports for the open month show the item flagged as belonging elsewhere."* That is a promised, visible flag — September's own report is supposed to show this Rs 3,500 marked as really belonging to August. It doesn't. `belongsToPeriodId` is absent from every report query in `api/src/queries/reports.ts` (each one filters exclusively on `postedPeriodId`), absent from every API response schema, and absent from all of `web/src` — never read back anywhere, only ever written. So September's report will blend this item into its ordinary total with no visible sign it's a late August fact. **Filed as GAP-173.** The lesson worth keeping, not just the finding: confirming that a *write* matches the spec is not the same claim as confirming the spec is *met* — F-8.1 makes two promises, and only checking the first one produced a wrong verdict in this document's own first pass.

## A note on the closed-period state, not a defect

This QA business's August 2026 accounting period was closed on 20 Aug 2026 by an earlier QA session testing period-close; September opened immediately after, per the normal close mechanism. That leaves "today" (23 Aug, this session's own business date) sitting inside a *closed* period while the *open* one is a month ahead — an unusual state, but one built up entirely by this business's own accumulated test history, not something a real, newly-onboarded business would ever be in (its first open period starts from its own go-live date and naturally includes "today" until the owner deliberately closes it). Worth knowing before reading `QA-SCEN-BUS1`'s own day-record dates literally, but not filed as a gap.
